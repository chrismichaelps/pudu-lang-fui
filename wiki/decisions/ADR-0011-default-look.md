# ADR-0011: The default look

## Decision

Controls are pills with filled surfaces and no border; cards are large, softly ringed surfaces; color
is a neutral scale. Sizes follow a 0.25rem unit: controls are 32, 36, and 40 pixels. Radii derive from
one base up to 2.6 times it. Muted text and the focus ring are slightly stronger than a purely
aesthetic choice would make them, so the contrast audit passes in both modes.
