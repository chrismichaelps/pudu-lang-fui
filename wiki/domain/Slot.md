# Slot

- **Definition:** The `data-slot` name every [[domain/Component]] and [[domain/Part]] renders. It is
  the selector its [[domain/Block]] styles, the hook a project's stylesheet targets, and how a page
  discovers which blocks it needs.
- **Rule:** A slot name belongs to exactly one block.
