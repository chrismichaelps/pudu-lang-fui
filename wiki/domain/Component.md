# Component

- **Definition:** One file holding a piece of interface: its [[domain/Props]], its markup, and its
  [[domain/Block]]. A function that answers an open node.
- **Canonical name:** Component. A sub-function of one is a [[domain/Part]].
- **Not:** A widget instance, a class, or a template.
- **Example:** `Button.button(Button.props().variant(Button.Outline)).says("Save")`.
