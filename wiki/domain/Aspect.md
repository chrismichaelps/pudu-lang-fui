# Aspect

- **Definition:** One of the seven independent dimensions of a [[domain/Theme]]: color, typography,
  spacing, shape, elevation, motion, and iconography. Each is a record of [[domain/Token]] values.
- **Rule:** Changing one aspect never requires changing another.

> ⚠ ASSUMPTION (escalated, defaulted): the brief names seven aspects of interface design without
> listing them. These seven are the dimensions a design system's tokens divide into; a different
> list changes only how the theme records are grouped.
