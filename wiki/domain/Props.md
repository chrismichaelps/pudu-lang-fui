# Props

- **Definition:** The record of what a [[domain/Component]] can be told. Built by `props()`, changed
  by a chain with one method per prop, or written as a record.
- **Canonical name:** Props, one field per prop; a closed choice is a [[domain/Variant]].
- **Not:** HTML attributes, which are added to the answered node with `.at`, `.id`, and `.data`.
