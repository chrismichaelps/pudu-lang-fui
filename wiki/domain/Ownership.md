# Ownership

- **Definition:** After `fui add`, a [[domain/Component]]'s source lives in the project under the
  project's module root, and the project edits it directly. `PuduLangFui.Core`, `Domain`, and `Theme`
  stay a dependency and keep receiving fixes.
